# Cycle 07 — navigable public reading

Status: **proposal, not canon**. Contribution is not truth, authority, ownership, publication or canon.

Date: 2026-10-30

This note follows [Cycle 06](CYCLE-06-CONSTELLATION.md). It records a public reading in which each room named there can be opened and read in one constellation. It does not cut DNS for `huihho.com`. It does not replace the edition already published there. Publishing a reading and pointing a domain remain different acts.

HUI is not a holding company and does not own these initiatives.

## What changed from Cycle 06

- Each intended host now has a readable room, with its evidence state and its boundary on the same page.
- The eight entrances of the published homepage lead to readings, not to services.
- `ggg.hiuhho.com` remains a spelling variant only. The intended host is `ggg.huihho.com`.
- Rooms without a host in Cycle 06 — regenerative living, and culture and play — are included as readings and are not given invented hosts.
- Six of the twelve themes are named, because the published edition names them: identity, knowledge, learning, economy, governance and territory. The other six are not invented here.

## What this file does not do

- It does not point DNS.
- It does not open accounts, treasuries, care services or assemblies.
- It does not invent a public expansion for CIESSM.
- It does not treat visual studies or private office folders as approved design.
- It does not move the published first edition (Home, About, H₂O WTV Café, Participation) off `huihho.com`.

Until whoever holds the domain points the hosts, the rooms are a reading. A reading can be visited. A reading is not a cutover.
